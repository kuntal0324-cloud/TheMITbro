Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Current status: checkout is disabled, so TheMITbro is not presently accepting orders or payments. No preorder is accepted for an unfinished paper or pack.

1. Before digital delivery

When checkout is eventually enabled, an order may be cancelled before payment completion. A failed or abandoned checkout does not create a right to download a product.

2. After digital delivery

Because a PDF can be consumed immediately after delivery, change-of-mind cancellation may not be available after successful access, subject always to rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. This does not limit review of a genuine payment or delivery problem.

3. Problems eligible for review

  • Duplicate payment for the same intended purchase.
  • Payment captured but no usable download was delivered.
  • A corrupted, inaccessible or wrong purchased file.
  • A delivered file materially different from the enabled product description.
  • Another remedy required by applicable law.

4. How to request help

Email themitbro.support@gmail.com with the order ID, payment ID if available, purchase email, date, product name and a clear description of the problem. Do not send a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, password or banking credential.

5. Review and remedy

TheMITbro plans to acknowledge a complete request within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month. Depending on verified circumstances, the remedy may be a working redelivery link, the correct replacement file, or a refund to the original payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing time may apply after a refund is initiated.

6. Seller and grievance details

Final refund timelines, legal seller details, escalation contacts and any payment-provider-specific procedures must be confirmed during legal and operational review before checkout is activated.
