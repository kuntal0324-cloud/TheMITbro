Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Current status: checkout is disabled and no paper is offered for sale. Verified seller, address, telephone and grievance-contact details still have to be published and reviewed before commerce may be activated.

1. Scope

This notice describes how TheMITbro handles information when you browse this website, contact support, or—after a future commercial launch—attempt to buy a digital educational product.

2. Information that may be handled

  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, diagnostic logs and security events may be processed by the hosting infrastructure.
  • Support data: if you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send.
  • Future checkout data: after checkout is activated, the site may handle your name, email address, product identifier, order and payment identifiers, amount, currency and payment status to verify a purchase and deliver the correct file.

TheMITbro does not ask you to send full card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, passwords or online-banking credentials. The current locked catalog does not load the Razorpay checkout script. If an eligible listing is activated later, that script will load only after you choose to purchase; Razorpay's own privacy practices will then also apply.

3. Why information is used

  • Operate, secure and troubleshoot the website.
  • Answer support, access and complaint requests.
  • After launch, verify payment status, prevent fraud and deliver the exact purchased product.
  • Keep transaction and compliance records where required.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by hosting, email, security and—after launch—payment providers only as needed to operate the service. It may also be disclosed when required by law, to investigate abuse, or to protect users and the service. TheMITbro does not claim to sell personal information.

5. Browser storage

The current development website uses local browser storage only to remember that you dismissed its development notice. Clearing site data removes that preference. Future analytics or advertising storage must not be introduced without updating this notice and any consent controls required for their use.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for support, security, transaction, accounting and legal needs, then deleted or anonymised when no longer required. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

7. Your requests

You may ask about personal information associated with your support or future order record, request correction, or raise a privacy concern by emailing themitbro.support@gmail.com. Include enough information to locate the relevant record, but never email payment secrets.

8. Details required before commercial launch

The verified legal seller name, principal geographic address, customer-care telephone number and grievance contact are not yet published. Their absence is a launch blocker, not permission to accept payments anonymously.
